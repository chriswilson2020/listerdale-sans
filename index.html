Designed for reading

Most reading difficulty starts with letters that look alike. This family removes the ambiguity at the source, then gives every word a little more room to breathe.

1lI

One, lowercase l and capital I each take a different shape, so codes and references read without guesswork.

quip

The q carries a tail on its descender, so p and q are no longer mirror twins.

wow

An open, uncrossed w. One less unfamiliar shape to decode.

0123456789

Lining figures align steadily in tables and hold their own beside capitals.

Why we made it

Our work runs on documents: proposals, reports, slide decks. If one reader in ten finds them harder to read than they should be, that is a communication problem, and we sit inside it. We wanted a typeface that removed the friction without looking like an aid. The ones that existed either announced themselves loudly or came with licence fees, so we drew our own and decided it should be free. If it makes one report easier for someone to read, releasing it was worth the effort.
